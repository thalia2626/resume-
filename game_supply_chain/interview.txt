================================================================================
INTERVIEW QUESTION BANK — 300 QUESTIONS
Chief Supply Chain Officer (CSCO) / VP / Director of Supply Chain
Albright Laboratories LLC
================================================================================
Purpose: screen for the RIGHT candidate — a proven, floor-to-executive supply
chain leader qualified on experience and results (no degree required, no clearance
required). Use as a bank: pull the sections that matter for the specific bid and
the specific candidate. Questions are numbered 1–300 for easy scoring.

How to score: look for (a) ownership, (b) QUANTIFIED results (%, $, time), (c)
hands-on command of the full chain, (d) how they develop the person behind them,
and (e) fit for a lean, price-disciplined small-business / federal-contracting org.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — CAREER PATH & EXPERIENCE-FIRST BACKGROUND (Q1–Q18)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Walk me through your career from your first supply chain job to today.
2.  What was your very first role in a warehouse, purchasing, or planning, and
    what did it teach you that you still use?
3.  You built your career from the floor up — which link of the chain do you
    understand best because you actually ran it yourself?
4.  Which link do you understand least, and how do you compensate as a leader?
5.  What promotion did you earn that you're proudest of, and what result got you
    it?
6.  Tell me about a time you were promoted into a role you weren't "ready" for on
    paper. How did you close the gap?
7.  How many years of progressive supply chain / operations leadership do you
    have, and how much of it included P&L or budget ownership?
8.  Describe the largest budget or managed spend you have owned end to end.
9.  What's the largest workforce you have led, directly and indirectly?
10. Why do you believe hands-on experience beats a classroom for this work?
11. What certifications do you hold (CPIM, CSCP, CLTD, Six Sigma, PMP), and how
    did each change how you actually run operations?
12. Which certification did you earn while working full-time, and what did it cost
    you to finish it?
13. What's a skill you taught yourself on the job that no course could have given
    you?
14. Tell me about a lateral move you made on purpose to learn a new link of the
    chain.
15. What would your first boss say about you? Your most recent one?
16. What made you leave your last two roles, in your own words?
17. Where do you want to be in five years, and does a CSCO role at a lean small
    business fit that?
18. What about this specific role and company made you apply?
